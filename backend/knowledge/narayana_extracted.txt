Narayana IIT Olympiad School, Jubilee Hills is part of the prestigious Narayana Group's
school network in Hyderabad. It blends academic rigour with holistic development, using
CBSE as the primary board while drawing from ICSE, IGCSE and IB frameworks in its
curriculum design. It is classified as a Day Boarding School — meaning students can
arrive early and stay late, with supervised study time available, without being full-time
residents.
